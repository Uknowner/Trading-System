My Trading System

1. News Filter▾

  • Do not trade during NFP.
  • Do not trade during any news alerts.
  • News comes first because it can destroy the setup.

2. Market Conditions▾

  • Do not trade during consolidation.
  • Do not trade inside messy ranging conditions.
  • Wait for cleaner expansion and confirmation.

3. Direction and Zones▾

  • Trade with the trend on the highest timeframe.
  • Use 4H and 1D zones as the main HTF context.
  • 1H is the main execution timeframe for EURUSD.
  • MTF context matters, especially 30m to 1H structure.

4. Confirmations▾

  • False tap + engulfing candle.
  • Ranging accumulation or distribution plus change in structure on the LTF.
  • Engulfing candle plus 50 EMA false tap on the MTF-LTF.
  • Weak engulfing is acceptable only if backed by other confirmations.

5. Engulfing Rule▾

  • Body only counts.
  • A long wick without a body is not an engulfing candle.
  • Engulfing should preferably break the previous LTF structure.
  • Engulfing can occur mid-zone.

6. Entry Rule▾

  • Wait for an A setup.
  • No chasing after a missed entry.
  • No countertrend revenge entries.
  • Only take clean setups that fit the full context.

7. Stop and Risk▾

  • Risk is based on a funded account around $10 minimum, about R100.
  • Risk can be 5% to 20% of the account balance.
  • Never take trades below 1:2 risk-to-reward.
  • Protect the account first.

8. Take Profit▾

  • TP must be below the next HTF zone.
  • Trade toward the trend and take profit before major opposing supply or resistance.
  • Do not aim through the next higher timeframe zone.
  • Use MTF consolidation and S&R to judge reversal risk.

9. Trade Avoidance Rules▾

  • No trading during news events.
  • No trading during consolidation.
  • No countertrend revenge entries.
  • No chasing after missed entry.

10. Final Rule▾

Stay with the trend, wait for A setups, respect higher timeframe zones, and protect the account so I stay in the game.
